## The Great Debate

This is the question we hear more than any other: "Should I run Google Ads or Meta Ads?"

The answer is not one or the other. It depends entirely on your business model, your audience, and your sales cycle. Let us break it down.

## Google Ads: Capturing Demand

Google Ads puts your business in front of people who are **actively searching** for what you sell. This is called demand capture.

When someone types "best web design agency in Jaipur" into Google, they have intent. They are ready to buy. Google Ads lets you appear at the very top of that search result.

### When to use Google Ads:
- Your audience knows they have a problem and is searching for a solution
- You sell a service or product people actively look for
- You want leads that are closer to making a buying decision
- You have a clear, definable set of keywords to target

### Typical performance:
- **Higher cost per click** (₹50–500+ depending on industry)
- **Higher conversion rate** (3-8% average)
- **Faster path to sale** (the lead already wants what you sell)

## Meta Ads: Creating Demand

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) puts your business in front of people who **did not know they needed you yet**. This is called demand creation.

Your ideal customer is scrolling through their Instagram feed, and your ad stops them mid-scroll with a compelling message. They had no intention of looking for your service, but now they are interested.

### When to use Meta Ads:
- Your product or service is visually compelling
- You want to build brand awareness at scale
- Your audience does not know they have a problem yet
- You want to reach people based on demographics and interests, not keywords

### Typical performance:
- **Lower cost per click** (₹5–50 average)
- **Lower initial conversion rate** (1-3%)
- **Requires nurturing** (retargeting sequences, email follow-ups)

## The Smart Approach: Use Both

The most profitable businesses we work with use **both platforms together** in a strategic funnel:

### Stage 1: Awareness (Meta Ads)
Run video and carousel ads to a cold audience. Goal: introduce your brand and drive traffic to educational content.

### Stage 2: Consideration (Meta Retargeting)
Retarget people who engaged with Stage 1 content. Show them case studies, testimonials, and specific service pages.

### Stage 3: Conversion (Google Ads)
Capture the people who are now actively searching for your service (thanks to the awareness you built with Meta). These leads convert at a much higher rate because they already know and trust your brand.
